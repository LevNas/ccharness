# Restraint during the discussion phase

When the user has said they want to think it over, hear opinions, or discuss, do not rush into implementation, large rewrites, or "next implementation steps".

## Apply

- Hold back proposals for implementation actions (new files, large rewrites, code, integration design).
- Prioritise laying out the issues, making trade-offs explicit, listing what is undecided, and deepening the idea.
- Avoid pre-emptive statements such as "I will update the notes"; say instead "once agreed, I will reflect it".
- Move to implementation only once agreement is confirmed.
- Light edits to discussion notes (adding a table, recording a confirmed fact) are fine; restructuring waits for agreement.

## Why

Without a deliberate switch between discussing and implementing, the next implementation step tends to surface right after a skeleton proposal, and the user has to push back with "we are still discussing".
